Our story

Fashion should be explored, not left unworn.

Hello, assessors! Welcome to {{ shop_name }}, created by Chuan Kang and Elgin.

We created this application because many members of Gen Z enjoy exploring their personal style but may hesitate to commit to purchasing an unfamiliar or statement piece. Clothing can be expensive, trends evolve quickly, and an item purchased for one occasion may remain unused afterwards.

{{ shop_name }} provides a business-to-consumer clothing rental marketplace where customers can discover garments from approved vendors, rent them for a chosen period, and return them after use. This gives customers greater freedom to experiment with fashion without needing to purchase every piece they wish to try.

The platform

More than a clothing catalogue

Vendors can list their clothing, manage physical stock, fulfil rental orders and monitor their rental performance. Customers can browse eligible listings, reserve available items, manage their rentals and review pieces they have completed renting.

To make fashion discovery more personal, customers can complete a style-preference quiz. A Random Forest machine learning model uses their answers to predict a preferred style, while wishlist, browsing and completed-rental interactions help rank relevant products on the For You page.

Platform administrators oversee vendors, marketplace activity, rental records, commissions and vendor payouts, helping to keep the marketplace organised and accountable.
