Your rental journey

Each item is tracked separately, so products within the same order may progress at different times.

Step 1

Confirmed

Your rental has been successfully placed.

Step 2

Preparing

The vendor is preparing your item.

Step 3

Out for delivery

Your item is on its way to you.

Step 4

Delivered

Your item has arrived and the rental is in progress.

Step 5

Returned

The item has been returned for inspection.

Step 6

Completed

Inspection and settlement are complete.

If an item is not returned by its due date, the vendor may mark the item as overdue.

Before you rent

A few things to know

Availability

Availability depends on your selected variant, physical stock and reservations for your chosen dates.

Rental pricing

Every product has a daily rental rate. Your total depends on the selected duration and quantity.

Cancellations

Eligible rental items can be cancelled while they are still confirmed or being prepared.
