Getting started

From registration to your first listing

Your company must be reviewed by a platform administrator before its products become publicly available.

  1. 1

    Register your company

    Provide your company name, contact information and address. You will also create the first manager account for your company.

  2. 2

    Wait for platform approval

    A platform administrator reviews your company registration. Your listings will remain hidden until the company is approved.

  3. 3

    Build your catalogue

    Add product descriptions, categories, daily rental rates, style tags, images, variants and physical stock quantities.

  4. 4

    Publish and fulfil rentals

    Publish eligible listings, process customer rental orders and monitor your company’s operational and financial performance.
